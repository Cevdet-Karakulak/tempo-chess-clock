## Description
Every game has its own rhythm. Tempo turns your iPhone or iPad into a two-player chess clock. Large touch targets and a clear active-player indicator make time easy to follow.

• Bullet, blitz, rapid and classical presets, plus fully adjustable time controls.
• Fischer increment, delay, Bronstein, hourglass, Japanese byo-yomi and Canadian overtime.
• Tournament periods with time additions at move thresholds. Each player's moves are counted independently, with unused time retained.
• Different main times and increments for simultaneous exhibition roles. Use one device per board.
• Player colors, ready-made palettes and custom color codes.
• A nostalgic flag indicator and optional flag-fall finish animation.
• Move log, thinking-time statistics, undo last press and referee time corrections.
• Share all recorded presses as PDF, or save the last 20 presses and game summary directly to Photos.
• Favorite time controls and Turkish/English language selection.
• Portrait and landscape layouts on iPhone and iPad.

No account, internet connection or ads are required. Settings and the last game stay on your device. Leaving the app or locking the screen pauses play; tap Resume when you return.

Tempo is a timer for physical board games. It does not evaluate piece moves or determine a chess position's result. Follow your event's device and time-control requirements for official tournament use.
